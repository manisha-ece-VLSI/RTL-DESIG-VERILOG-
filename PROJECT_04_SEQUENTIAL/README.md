# PROJECT 04 - Sequential Circuits
Design of Latches and Flip-Flops.

## Modules
- `d_latch`, `d_ff` - D Latch & D Flip-Flop
- `s_r_latch`, `s_r_ff` - SR Latch & SR FF
- `j_k_latch`, `j_k_ff` - JK Latch & JK FF
- `t_latch`, `t_ff` - T Latch & T FF

## Concepts
Latch vs Flip-Flop, Level vs Edge triggering, SR/JK/D/T behavior
